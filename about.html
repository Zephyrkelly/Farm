About Duskamz Farms

Duskamz Farms is a growing agribusiness dedicated to providing fresh livestock, eggs, catfish, and Black Soldier Fly (BSF) feed. Our mission is to combine sustainable farming practices with modern technology to deliver healthy, affordable products to our community.

Founded with a vision to reduce waste and create value, we are committed to innovation in agriculture and empowering local farmers. Every product we offer reflects our passion for quality and sustainability.

We believe farming is more than just business, it’s a way to nourish families, strengthen communities, and protect the environment for future generations.
